Help for merchants

Card terminal connection is still in trial and not yet available to every shop.

Connect your card terminal to PolyPOS

PolyPOS sends the amount to your bank's card terminal and gets the result back by itself, so nothing is keyed in twice. The two are connected through Linkly Cloud.

Before you start

  • Your terminal must support Linkly Cloud and be connected to the internet through your shop's Wi-Fi or a network cable. Check Linkly's list of supported terminals or ask your bank.
  • Ask your bank to turn on Linkly Cloud for your terminal. The bank emails you a Linkly Cloud username and password.
  • You need to be able to sign in to the PolyPOS Dashboard as the owner.

Pair the terminal (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open the PolyPOS Dashboard and go to Card terminal.
  2. Enter the Linkly Cloud username and password from your bank's email.
  3. On the card terminal, press FUNC, enter 8880, then press OK (or Enter). The terminal shows a pairing code. The code works for 3 minutes. If your terminal has no FUNC key or shows something else, ask your bank how to get a Linkly Cloud pairing code.
  4. Enter the pairing code in PolyPOS and press Pair. You'll see Paired.
  5. On the same Card terminal page, use Try a payment for a small amount, then Refund this, to check everything works.

If something goes wrong

“Linkly turned it down”

Check the username and password. The pairing code changes after 3 minutes, so make a new one (FUNC → 8880 → OK) and enter it straight away.

The page says “Pair again: …”

This shows when the terminal's pairing code was reset, the Linkly Cloud password changed, or the terminal was paired with another POS. Pair it again as above.

The terminal is offline

The terminal must stay connected to the internet. If it is offline, a payment waits for a while and then shows Not sent, or Result unknown (see below).

“Result unknown”

If PolyPOS can't reach Linkly, it may not know whether a payment went through. Look at the terminal's screen or its receipt, then choose Approved or Declined in PolyPOS.

Don't just charge the card again when the result is unknown. Check the terminal first, or the customer could be charged twice.

If the internet is down

If your terminal can still reach the bank on its own (for example over mobile data; ask your bank), take the payment on the terminal by itself, then record it in PolyPOS as a Card payment.
